National recognition

Top 3 across India — USO@75 AI Innovation Challenge

Students of Birla Balika Vidyapeeth, Pilani took part in the USO@75 AI Innovation Challenge, organised by the United Schools Organisation (USO) of India as part of National Technology Day celebrations — and were placed among the Top 3 teams nationally for developing an AI-powered Career Navigation App, the same line of thinking that shaped this platform's four-rung learner journey.

This result reflects the same team's approach to AI-assisted, learner-first product design carried into the Skill India Digital Hub.

From the ladder

Learners who climbed all four rungs.

Illustrative profiles showing how the Learn → Practice → Certify → Employ path plays out across different sectors.

AK

Anita Kumari

Web Development Track

Anita worked in a small stitching unit and taught herself basic computer use on a shared family phone. Within twelve weeks on the Web Development track, she went from her first HTML page to deploying a working portfolio site, and was placed as a junior support developer with one of the Hub's hiring partners.

"I didn't need a laptop or a coaching class near me — just a phone, a plan, and someone checking my work."

RS

Ravi Sonar

Electrical Fundamentals Track

Ravi had years of informal electrical experience but no certificate to show for it. The Electrical Fundamentals track let him formalize what he already knew, close a few safety gaps, and pass the proctored assessment — turning years of word-of-mouth jobs into a verifiable qualification.

"The certificate didn't teach me everything from zero. It proved what I already knew how to do."

PM

Priya Meena

Community Health Worker Track

Priya wanted to work in her village's primary health center but lacked formal training. The Community Health Worker track combined structured lessons with supervised practice hours, and she now supports patient records and outreach visits for her local clinic.

"Every rung had a clear next step. I always knew exactly what came after."

DV

Deepak Verma

Precision Farming Track

Deepak manages his family's two-acre farm and wanted to reduce water waste. The Precision Farming Basics track walked him through soil testing and irrigation planning using tools he could access from a basic smartphone, cutting his irrigation costs within one growing season.

"I expected a course made for people with more land than me. This one wasn't."
